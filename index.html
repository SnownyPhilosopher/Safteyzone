History

Safety Zone Security Services Limited was formed in 2004 to fill the gap that existed in the security industry for an efficient, customer-focused security services provider. It was the founder's firm belief that the larger players had rolled out services that were not orientated towards each customer's needs — the principle of "one jacket fits all" doesn't work. Safety Zone's philosophy is to deploy systems and services that suit each individual client.
